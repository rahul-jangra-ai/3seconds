## 3. Right Column: The 3-Second Brief Form
- **Form Fields:**
  1. `Name` (Text, required)
  2. `Brand / Company Name` (Text, required)
  3. `Work Email` (Email, required)
  4. `Phone / WhatsApp` (Tel, required)
  5. `Monthly Marketing Budget Tier:`
     - `[ ] $5,000 - $15,000 (Testing the Waters)`
     - `[ ] $15,000 - $50,000 (Full Viral Campaign)`
     - `[ ] $50,000+ (Enterprise Omnichannel Syndicate)`
  6. `What is your primary goal?`
     - `[ ] Viral Brand Awareness`
     - `[ ] High-Converting Performance Ads (ROAS)`
     - `[ ] Creator / Meme Network Takeover`
  7. `Tell us about your project & biggest bottleneck` (Textarea, required)
- **Submit Button:** Kinetic button with magnetic hover: `"LAUNCH CAMPAIGN →"`
- **Success State:** Instant celebratory micro-animation with message: *"Hook received! Our strategists will reply within 3 hours."*
