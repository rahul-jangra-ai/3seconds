# 3seconds — Work & Case Studies Page Specification (`/work`)

## 1. Strategy & Presentation
The portfolio page must prove that creative wildness produces undeniable business revenue. Each case study combines high-definition looping video clips with verified analytics.

---

## 2. Interactive Filter Bar
- Categories: `[ All Work (24) ]`, `[ Viral Memes (12) ]`, `[ E-Commerce Hooks (8) ]`, `[ Entertainment & Films (6) ]`, `[ Tech & Fintech (5) ]`
- Filter animation: Smooth GSAP Flip transition repositioning cards on category selection without page reloads.

---

## 3. Case Study Card Structure
Each card features:
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or 9:16 vertical card with rounded corners (`16px`).
- **Media Asset:** Video thumbnail that seamlessly plays a 3-second looped teaser on mouse hover.
- **Client & Campaign Title:** E.g., *"Myntra: The 3-Second Wardrobe Meltdown"*.
- **Impact Metrics Pills:**
  - `👁️ 42M Organic Views`
  - `⚡ 88% 3-Second Hook Retention`
  - `📈 3.8x ROAS Multiplier`
- **Hook Strategy Teaser:** A single punchy sentence describing the core hook technique used.

---

## 4. Case Study Detail View (`/work/[slug]`)
Detailed modal or dedicated page including:
1. **Executive Summary & Brief:** The business challenge.
2. **The 3-Second Hypothesis:** What hook variation unlocked viral sharing.
3. **Asset Gallery:** Interactive video players showing Hook A vs Hook B vs Hook C.
4. **Data Breakdown:** Retention graphs, view-through rates, and audience comments wall.
5. **Client Quote & Verdict.**
