# 3seconds — About Page Specification (`/about`)

## 1. Page Narrative
The About page articulates the core conviction of 3seconds: **Attention is the rarest asset on earth.**
It contrasts traditional slow-paced advertising agencies with 3seconds' agile, internet-native culture.

---

## 2. Section Breakdown

### Section 1: Hero — The 3-Second Manifesto
- **Headline:** *"3 Seconds to Stop the Thumb. 30 Seconds to Build a Legend."*
- **Sub-headline:** Traditional agencies spend 6 months planning a 60-second TV commercial nobody watches. We build cultural hooks tested on millions of real humans in real time.
- **Visual Element:** Interactive split screen comparing "Traditional Ad View-Through Rate" (dropoff at second 2) vs. "3seconds Hook Retention" (90%+ retention through second 15).

### Section 2: Origin Story & Milestones
- **Layout:** Asymmetric 2-column narrative with floating sticky year markers (`2021`, `2023`, `2025`, `2026`).
- **Milestone Metrics:**
  - `500M+` Total Organic Views Generated
  - `1,200+` Viral Campaigns Executed
  - `350+` Creator Partnerships Managed
  - `94%` Average 3-Second Hook Retention Rate

### Section 3: The 3-Second Operating System (Framework)
1. **The Hook (0s - 3s):** Auditory disruptor, pattern interrupt, visual curiosity gap.
2. **The Hold (3s - 15s):** Fast-paced value delivery, rhythmic micro-cuts, dynamic subtitles.
3. **The Payoff (15s - 30s):** Emotional resolution, unexpected meme twist, organic brand integration.
4. **The Action (30s+):** Low-friction CTA, comment-section spark, viral shareability.

### Section 4: Leadership & Creative Disruptors
- Card grid featuring founders, creative directors, and meme strategists.
- Hover effect: Card flips or displays an embedded 3-second looping portrait video of each team member.

### Section 5: Anti-Boring Culture & Principles
- 10 Rules of 3seconds (e.g., *"Never make an ad that feels like an ad"*, *"If it doesn't make someone laugh, replay, or argue in the comments, rewrite it"*).
