# 3seconds — Services Page Specification (`/services`)

## 1. Capabilities Matrix
A full breakdown of the 6 core pillars of 3seconds:

### 1. Short-Form Viral Video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)
- **What it is:** High-energy vertical video engineered specifically for algorithm amplification.
- **Deliverables:** Conceptualization, scripting, casting, high-speed production, sound design, hook variations A/B testing.
- **Key Metric:** Hook retention rate, average percentage viewed (APV > 85%).

### 2. Meme Marketing & Cultural Seeding
- **What it is:** Turning brands into viral conversation starters using internet humor, Twitter/X trends, and relatable meme templates.
- **Deliverables:** Daily trend-jacking war room, custom meme formats, distribution across 100M+ meme page networks.
- **Key Metric:** Organic shares, saves, unprompted quote-tweets, earned media value (EMV).

### 3. High-Velocity Ad Production (0-30s Commercials)
- **What it is:** Modern, cinematic direct-response ads built for Meta Ads, TikTok Spark Ads, and YouTube Shorts Ads.
- **Deliverables:** 5 distinct 3-second hook variations per asset, dynamic motion graphics, behavioral psychology triggers.
- **Key Metric:** Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), Click-Through Rate (CTR > 3.5%), Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).

### 4. Creator Syndicate & Influencer Co-Creation
- **What it is:** Partnering with internet creators not for boring sponsored scripts, but for authentic cultural skits.
- **Deliverables:** Creator matchmaking, creative briefs, review & compliance, collaborative posting.
- **Key Metric:** Engagement rate, comment sentiment, brand recall.

### 5. Twitter/X Trend Engineering & Narrative Control
- **What it is:** Strategic hashtag deployment, quote-tweet momentum, and crisis neutralization.
- **Deliverables:** Live trend monitoring, influencer tweet cascades, organic thread development.
- **Key Metric:** Trending topics ranking (#1-#5 nationally), impressions volume.

### 6. YouTube Ecosystems & Retention Editing
- **What it is:** End-to-end strategy for YouTube channels: high-CTR thumbnails, first-60-second retention edits, YouTube Shorts funnels.
- **Deliverables:** Thumbnail A/B packaging, retention heat-map editing, community tab syndication.
- **Key Metric:** 24-hour VPH (Views Per Hour), Click-Through Rate (CTR > 10%).
