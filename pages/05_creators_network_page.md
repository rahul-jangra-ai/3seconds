# 3seconds — Creator Syndicate Page Specification (`/creators`)

## 1. Overview
3seconds manages and collaborates with a private syndicate of 250+ internet creators and 100+ high-traffic meme pages with an aggregate reach exceeding **600+ Million followers**.

---

## 2. Key Page Features

### A. Audience Reach Infographic
- Interactive counter displaying total network reach by region and demographic:
  - Gen-Z (18-24): 62%
  - Young Millennials (25-34): 31%
  - Male / Female Distribution: 52% / 48%
  - Primary Channels: Instagram (58%), YouTube Shorts (24%), Twitter/X (18%)

### B. Interactive Creator Roster Grid
- Filterable by Niche:
  - `Meme Pages & Satire` (e.g. Sarcastic_us, Ghantaa, TheIndianIdiot)
  - `Comedy & Skit Creators`
  - `Tech & Gadget Reviewers`
  - `Fashion & Aesthetics`
  - `Regional Creators (Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu)`
- Creator Cards show:
  - Profile Avatar & Verified Badge
  - Platform Icons with live follower counts (e.g., `14.7M`)
  - Signature Hook Style: "Awkward POV", "Fast-Cut Sarcasm", "Surrealist Voiceover"
  - Average Engagement Rate

### C. Creator Intake Portal ("Join the Syndicate")
- Lightweight modal form for creators with >50k followers to apply for brand campaign allocations.
