# Section Spec 08 — Client Partner Infinite Marquee ("Our Besties")

## 1. Concept: "The Best Work Isn't Made for Clients. It's Made with Partners."
A high-energy, multi-row infinite logo marquee demonstrating the agency's industry credibility across top consumer brands, entertainment giants, and tech unicorns.

---

## 2. Layout & Motion Specifications
- **Row 1:** Marquee scrolling from Left to Right at `40px/s`.
- **Row 2:** Marquee scrolling from Right to Left at `35px/s`.
- **Row 3 (Optional on desktop):** Marquee scrolling from Left to Right at `45px/s`.
- **Hover Interaction:** Hovering anywhere on a row pauses or slows down the ticker by 50%, with individual brand logos scaling up with full color saturation (default state: tasteful monochrome greyscale).

---

## 3. Brand Partner Lineup
- **E-Commerce & Retail:** Myntra, Amazon miniTV, Flipkart, Nykaa, CaratLane.
- **FMCG & Food:** KFC, Britannia Treat, Balaji Wafers, Vi-John, Svish.
- **Tech & Entertainment:** JioHotstar, Zee5, Noise, Oppo, Red Bull, Spotify.
- **Fintech & Gaming:** My11Circle, Dream11, CRED, Jupiter.
