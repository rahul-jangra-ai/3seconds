# Section Spec 06 — Creator Network Constellation & Orbital Grid

## 1. Concept: "Our Buddies / The Syndicate"
Brands don't just buy ad slots; they tap into our living, breathing network of creators and meme channels who own the cultural conversation.

---

## 2. Interactive Layout (Split Desktop)

### Left Side: Orbital Category Constellation
- An interactive SVG or Canvas diagram featuring a glowing center node labelled **`3SECONDS CORE`**.
- Satellites orbit around the core representing category nodes:
  - `[ Premium Memes ]`
  - `[ Gen-Z Creators ]`
  - `[ Regional Voices (Hindi, Punjabi, South) ]`
  - `[ Pop Culture & Entertainment ]`
  - `[ High-Energy Skits ]`
- Clicking any orbital node filters the cards on the right side in real time with an animated physics pulse.

### Right Side: Creator Metric Cards
- Cards feature:
  - Creator Avatar with glowing status ring
  - Channel / Page Handle (e.g. `@sarcastic_us`, `@ghantaa`, `@clips`, `@adultsociety`)
  - Verified Follower Count Badge (e.g. `14.7M Followers`, `10.1M Followers`, `8.0M Followers`)
  - Signature Hook Genre: "Dark Satire", "Relatable College Memes", "Bollywood Spin"
  - Primary Platform Badges: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X/Twitter
