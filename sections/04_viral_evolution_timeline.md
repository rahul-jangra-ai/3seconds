# Section Spec 04 — Viral Evolution Timeline (Horizontal Scroll)

## 1. Concept & Narrative
The **Evolution of the Hook & Meme** timeline documents how human attention shifted from 30-minute TV broadcasts to 6-second Vine clips, to 3-second TikTok/Reels hooks, and now AI-native micro-content.
It demonstrates that 3seconds understands internet culture better than any legacy agency.

---

## 2. Motion & Scrolling Mechanism
- As the user scrolls vertically into the section, the container pins in place (`pin: true, scrub: 1`).
- The vertical wheel movement translates directly to horizontal translateX movement across the timeline track.
- A glowing horizontal timeline progress bar tracks current year progression.

---

## 3. Timeline Milestone Cards

1. **2012 — The Dawn of Global Virality**
   - *Gangnam Style & Harlem Shake:* The first time internet choreography broke traditional broadcast media records.
2. **2015 — The 6-Second Vine Revolution**
   - *Vine & Peak Micro-Comedy:* Proving that complete narratives could be delivered in under 6 seconds.
3. **2018 — The Relatable Brown Boy & Meme Surge**
   - *Sacred Games & Zakir Khan ("Yahan Main Pighal Gaya"):* Local Indian pop culture transforms into universal meme templates.
4. **2020 — The Lockdown Hyper-Viral Era**
   - *Rasode Me Kaun Tha & Binod:* Sound-remixes and single-word comments dominating national news.
5. **2022 — The Absurdist Era**
   - *Puneet Superstar & Kacha Badam:* Raw, unpolished, user-generated eccentricity outperforming polished corporate commercials.
6. **2024 — The Micro-Hook Renaissance**
   - *So Beautiful So Elegant, Chin Tapak Dam Dam, Chill Guy:* The 3-second audio hook becomes the ultimate algorithmic catalyst.
7. **2026+ — The 3seconds Era**
   - *AI-Synthesized Viral Engineering:* Predictive hook analytics, instant culture adaptation, and zero-dropoff retention.
