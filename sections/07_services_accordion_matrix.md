## 1. Visual Style: Hollow-to-Solid Kinetic Typography
Inspired by the high-impact typography of *Creativefuel.io*, the services section presents capabilities in massive display typography.
- **Default (Collapsed):** Giant stroked/hollow outlined text (`color: transparent; -webkit-text-stroke: 1.5px #121212;`).
- **Active / Hovered (Expanded):** The font smoothly animates to solid filled text (`#121212` or `#FFFFFF` in dark mode) while an accordion drawer unfolds below revealing detailed strategic breakdowns.
